Word Game

Level & badges

Browsers ship several voices and the default one is often the most robotic-sounding. Try a few and pick whichever sounds most natural on this device.

Get more voices on this device
  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
  2. Pick a language, e.g. English → United Kingdom.
  3. Tap a voice name (Serena, Stephanie, Kate...) and download the Enhanced or Premium quality version — the default "Standard" ones are the robotic-sounding ones.
  4. Fully quit and reopen the browser (or restart the phone) after downloading, then tap the ↻ button above to re-check — newly downloaded voices often don't show up until then.

Siri voices don't show up here — this is a known Safari/iOS limitation (Siri's voices aren't available to web pages at all, however long you wait), not something this page can fix. The Enhanced/Premium voices above are the best-sounding ones actually available here.
